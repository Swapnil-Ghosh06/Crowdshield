# CrowdShield — Free Public Crowd Video Datasets & Test Resources
# ==============================================================================
# The datasets and sample video sources below can be used for benchmarking
# and testing the crowd density and flow estimation module.
# ==============================================================================

1. ShanghaiTech Crowd Dataset (Academic / Benchmark)
   - Description: Large-scale crowd dataset with 1,198 annotated images and video sequences with high-density crowd scenes.
   - Part A: High-density crowds (average density ~500 people per frame).
   - Part B: Medium-density urban surveillance scenes (average density ~120 people per frame).
   - Kaggle Dataset Mirror: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tthien/shanghaitech-with-people-density-map
   - Alternative Mirror: https://github.com/leeyeehoo/CSRNet-pytorch#data-preparation

2. Mall Dataset (Surveillance Crowd Video & Flow)
   - Description: Video sequence recorded by a publicly mounted surveillance CCTV camera in a shopping mall with 2,000 video frames at 640x480 resolution.
   - Ideal for: Low to medium density tracking and flow speed evaluation.
   - URL: https://personal.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/~ccloy/downloads_mall_dataset.html
   - Direct Data: http://personal.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/~ccloy/project_target_code/mall_dataset.rar

3. PETS 2009 Benchmark Dataset (Crowd Flow & Incident Detection)
   - Description: Multi-camera surveillance benchmark sequence S1 (Density Estimation and Flow) and S2 (People Tracking / Crowd Flow).
   - URL: http://www.cvg.reading.ac.uk/PETS2009/a.html
   - Video Sequence S1 (Crowd Flow): http://www.cvg.reading.ac.uk/PETS2009/data/S1/

4. UCF-QNRF Crowd Dataset
   - Description: Extremely dense crowd scenes recorded at concerts, religious gatherings, and stadiums.
   - URL: https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/data/ucf-qnrf/

5. Pexels Royalty-Free Public Crowd CCTV & Street Footage (Direct MP4 Downloads)
   - Urban pedestrian crossing & stadium egress videos suitable for quick offline CLI testing:
     - Egress crowd walking: https://www.pexels.com/video/crowd-of-people-walking-in-the-street-854745/
     - Busy railway station concourse: https://www.pexels.com/video/people-walking-at-the-subway-station-3015511/
     - Festival / concert crowd: https://www.pexels.com/video/crowd-at-a-concert-852423/
